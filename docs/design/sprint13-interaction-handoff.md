# Sprint 13 — Flash-event interactions

Reuse the PLM Quiet Heritage layouts, single feed and four attendee destinations.
Flash is an event type, not a new navigation section, Featured badge or animation.

The existing draft editor offers Scheduled / Flash. Choosing Flash explicitly
sets instant registration and clears the review window. Explain that Flash starts
within 24 hours of publication; preserve incomplete draft saves and all existing
required publication fields. Normal event title prominence, artwork, tags,
coarse area, preview, autosave and conflict handling stay in place. Published
Flash remains read-only except the established material-change workflow.

Use a restrained labeled Flash event badge in discovery, details and preview.
The type badge remains when temporary priority expires. For You may separately
show a controlled Flash priority in your area reason during an active server
match. Do not show private areas, distances, GPS, countdown promises or fake
urgency. Keep club groups ahead of non-club Flash matches and keep explicit
Upcoming/Newest chronological. Refresh visible For You periodically using the
existing polling helper; server boundaries are exact, display may lag up to 30 s.

Show Flash events is a labeled saved switch in discovery, on by default. Disable
until the current preference scope loads. Account choices stay with the account;
guests use existing device preferences. Hide filters discovery before pagination
and composes with search, sort and My Clubs. It does not cancel registration,
hide My Events/detail links or change chat/notifications. Do not call it a mute
because no notification muting is implemented. A reset restores the default on.

Maintain concise accessible names, separate helper descriptions, gold focus,
wrap-safe English/Filipino and large text, high contrast and reduced motion.
Keep management desktop-only. Record actual Edge interaction and viewport
evidence separately from participant, assistive-technology and native-device
validation. No Penpot synchronization, cloud or deployment is included.
