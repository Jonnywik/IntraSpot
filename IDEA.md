# IntraSpot

An event-discovery and registration service for PLM-recognized student organizations and current PLM students/faculty. Public visitors may browse. PLM Gmail addresses using `.plm.edu.ph` establish current status and gate registration/social and organization membership/workspace access. IntraSpot platform admins assess only the organization's accreditation with the academy during an offline review; no additional evidence is checked. They decide approval based on their assessment. Organizations may draft while pending and publish only after approval. In-app decision records capture the outcome, deciding platform admin, and decision date/time for each approval or rejection; event geography remains unresolved.

Current product decisions live in the Obsidian project hub at `Hermes/Projects/IntraSpot.md`. Early brainstorm concepts are not requirements unless explicitly adopted in the project notes.
